Check the can number, actual view, and red crop box for each included image, then mark it reviewed. Gaps, extra images and similar cans can shift later numbering. Red outline is the proposed crop, not a verified object boundary.

The blue line marks the chosen center of the can. Use improved detected centers restores the new automatic estimates, including after loading a previous review plan. Click its center in a photo, or enter Can center X, then use Center crop. Center all crops uses the current centers for all included photos. The crop width and height are preserved; at an image edge the crop may not be fully centered. Crop margin is background space per side as a percentage of the detected can width and height: 0 is tighter, 20 leaves more space, and 40 leaves still more. Set the margin and apply it to all included photos or to an individual photo. This replaces existing crop adjustments for affected photos and clears Reviewed when a crop changes. Photos with no detected bounds are skipped. Inspect every crop: the detected bounds may miss part of a can. Requested background can be limited by the original photo edges. Adjust crop coordinates in pixels on the correctly oriented image. Uncheck Include to exclude an image completely from output. The top Check all selected as reviewed button checks every included photo at once. The group Reviewed button checks or unchecks that group’s selected photos; excluded photos stay unchecked. Check any focus warnings and unresolved view letters before producing output. Focus warnings indicate low image detail, which can result from blur or a plain surface; inspect them and do not assume unflagged images are sharp. Enlarged focus detail is provided for photos flagged during analysis; newly flagged photos after changing the threshold may require opening the original. Groups are only a visual aid: edit the can number and letter on each card to handle mistakes or mixed sequences. Download reviewed plan includes only photos that are both included and Reviewed; others are omitted. To preserve every decision and resume unfinished work, use Save work in progress. Changes remain in this browser only until you save a file. Create a size profile with output width, height and background, then assign the same profile to every view of a can. Can types are chosen by you; the program does not identify them automatically. Each profile fits the whole crop into a fixed canvas without distortion; unenlarged crops may have extra background. Size profiles and the contrast setting apply to Python-cropped JPEGs, not byte-identical copies for PSP; Preview output shows an approximation from the thumbnail. Each RGB channel is stretched independently, which may change color; the stronger setting clips 0.5% of pixels at each end. No photos or data are uploaded.
